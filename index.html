ABOUT US

Our Story & Mission

Founded with deep commitment to neurodivergent children and their families

At Butterfly Bloom Child Development Center, we believe every child has the potential to flourish—they just need the right environment and support to bloom at their own unique pace.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide compassionate, evidence-based therapy services that empower children with autism, ADHD, and developmental delays to reach their full potential while supporting families every step of the way.

💡 Our Philosophy

Every child is unique. Every family's journey is different. We celebrate neurodiversity and focus on building on each child's strengths while gently supporting areas of growth.

🤝 Our Approach

We take a holistic, family-centered approach. We don't just work with children—we partner with parents, caregivers, and educators to create consistent support systems.

🌟 Our Team's Expertise

Our team brings together professionals with diverse experience from Visakhapatnam, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kerala, and international exposure from the USA and UK. We've worked with over 500 families in and around Visakhapatnam, bringing the latest therapeutic approaches and genuine passion for helping children thrive.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

What age groups do you work with?

+

We work with children from 18 months to 14 years old. Early intervention makes a significant difference, but we're here to support children at any stage of their development journey.

How do I know if my child needs therapy?

+

If you've noticed delays in speech, difficulty with social interactions, challenges with daily activities, sensory sensitivities, or behavioral concerns, a consultation can help. Trust your instincts—you know your child best.

Are your therapists qualified and certified?

+

Our team brings together professionals registered with the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI), with diverse experience from Vizag, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kerala, and even international exposure from the USA and UK. We've worked with over 500 families in and around Visakhapatnam.

Do you offer online therapy sessions?

+

Yes! We provide both in-person and online therapy sessions. Online sessions work particularly well for parent training, speech therapy, and behavioral consultations, making quality care accessible no matter where you are.

How long does therapy take?

+

Every child's journey is unique. Some children make rapid progress in a few months, while others benefit from longer-term support. We regularly assess progress and adjust our approach to ensure we're meeting your child's evolving needs.

How much does therapy cost?

+

We create personalized therapy plans based on your child's specific needs and your family's goals. We offer flexible monthly packages to make therapy more accessible. Many families also find that their insurance providers offer reimbursement for therapy services. Contact us to discuss what might work best for your situation.

Can parents observe therapy sessions?

+

Absolutely! We encourage parent involvement. You're welcome to observe sessions, and we'll regularly share strategies you can use at home to reinforce your child's progress.

What should I bring to the first appointment?

+

Bring any previous assessment reports, medical records, school reports, and a list of your concerns and goals. Most importantly, bring your questions—we're here to listen and support you.
