Our team of RCI registered therapists brings together expertise in speech therapy, occupational therapy, ABA therapy, and special education to create individualized treatment plans that deliver real, measurable results.

Services We Offer

Comprehensive therapeutic services for children with developmental needs

🗣️

Speech Therapy

Expert treatment for speech delays, articulation disorders, language development, stuttering, and communication challenges. Our RCI registered speech therapists use play-based and evidence-based approaches.

Learn More →
🎨

Occupational Therapy

Comprehensive OT services for fine motor skills, sensory integration, self-care activities, handwriting, and daily living skills. Specialized sensory gym and equipment available.

Learn More →
🧩

ABA Therapy

Applied Behavior Analysis for children with autism spectrum disorder. Structured programs focusing on communication, social skills, adaptive behaviors, and reducing challenging behaviors.

Learn More →
📚

Special Education

Customized learning programs for children with learning disabilities, intellectual disabilities, and developmental delays. Focus on academic skills, cognitive development, and school readiness.

Learn More →
👶

Early Intervention

Critical developmental support for infants and toddlers (0-3 years). Early identification and intervention for developmental delays and disabilities.

Learn More →
🎯

Behavioral Therapy

Positive behavior support and management strategies for challenging behaviors. Parent training and consultation for consistent implementation at home.

Learn More →
🤝

Social Skills Training

Group and individual sessions to develop peer interaction, conversation skills, emotional regulation, and friendship-building abilities.

Learn More →
💬

Parent Counseling

Guidance and support for parents navigating their child's developmental journey. Training on home-based strategies and emotional support.

Learn More →
📋

Developmental Assessment

Comprehensive evaluations to identify strengths and areas of need. Standardized testing and detailed reports for treatment planning.

Learn More →
🎒

School Readiness

Preparation programs for mainstream school integration. Focus on academic readiness, classroom behavior, and independence skills.
