Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our therapies and services

What is Speech Therapy and when should my child start?

+

Speech therapy helps children develop communication skills, including speaking, understanding language, and social communication. It addresses speech delays, articulation problems, stuttering, and language disorders.

Start therapy if your child: Isn't speaking by age 2, has unclear speech by age 3, struggles to follow directions, has difficulty expressing needs, or shows frustration when communicating.

✅ Early intervention is key! The earlier you start, the better the outcomes. Our RCI-registered speech therapists in Visakhapatnam create personalized programs for each child.

Does online therapy work as effectively as in-person sessions?

+

Yes! Online therapy is highly effective and research shows excellent outcomes when delivered by qualified professionals. At Butterfly Bloom CDC, we offer both in-person and online therapy options.

Benefits of Online Therapy:

✅ Convenience: No travel time, flexible scheduling, therapy from home comfort
✅ Consistency: Fewer missed sessions due to weather, illness, or travel
✅ Parent Involvement: Parents actively participate and learn techniques
✅ Natural Environment: Children practice skills in their daily setting
✅ Technology Engagement: Kids often respond well to screen-based activities

🌟 Our Success Rate: 85% of families report significant progress with online therapy. We use interactive tools, games, and activities that keep children engaged throughout sessions.

Best for: Speech therapy, special education tutoring, parent counseling, and follow-up sessions. Some hands-on therapies like sensory integration work best in-person initially, then can transition online.

What is Occupational Therapy (OT) and how does it help children?

+

Occupational Therapy helps children develop skills needed for daily activities - from self-care to school tasks. It addresses fine motor skills, sensory processing, handwriting, and independence.

OT helps with: Difficulty holding pencils, poor handwriting, trouble with buttons/zippers, sensory sensitivities (textures, sounds), coordination problems, attention difficulties, and daily routine challenges.

💪 Real Results: Children gain confidence, independence, and skills for school success. Our OT programs in Visakhapatnam use play-based activities that make therapy fun and effective.

What is ABA Therapy and is it only for autism?

+

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is a scientific approach to understanding and changing behavior. While commonly used for autism, it benefits many developmental conditions.

ABA helps with: Communication skills, social interactions, reducing challenging behaviors, learning new skills, attention and focus, following instructions, and daily living skills.

Who benefits: Children with autism spectrum disorder, ADHD, developmental delays, behavioral challenges, and learning disabilities.

🎯 Evidence-Based: ABA is the gold standard for autism intervention with decades of research supporting its effectiveness. Our certified therapists create individualized programs based on each child's needs.

How long does therapy take to show results?

+

Timeline varies by child and condition, but most families notice improvements within 3-6 months of consistent therapy.

Factors affecting progress: Severity of condition, age at start, consistency of sessions, home practice, and family involvement.

Typical timelines:
• Speech delays: 6-12 months for significant improvement
• Occupational therapy: 3-9 months for skill development
• ABA therapy: 6-18 months for behavioral changes
• Special education: Ongoing support with quarterly progress

📊 We track progress: Regular assessments every 3 months show measurable improvements. Parents receive detailed progress reports and home activity recommendations.

What is Sensory Integration Therapy?

+

Sensory Integration Therapy helps children who struggle to process sensory information (touch, sound, sight, movement, taste, smell). It's crucial for children with sensory processing disorder.

Signs your child needs sensory therapy: Oversensitive to textures/sounds/lights, seeks excessive movement, poor coordination, difficulty with transitions, meltdowns in busy environments, picky eating, or avoids messy play.

🎨 Our Approach: We use specialized equipment (swings, weighted items, textured materials) in a sensory gym environment. Children learn to regulate responses and feel more comfortable in daily situations.

Can my child attend regular school while receiving therapy?

+

Absolutely! Most children continue regular school while receiving therapy. In fact, therapy often helps children succeed better in school.

We offer:
• Flexible scheduling (after school, weekends)
• School collaboration and teacher consultations
• IEP (Individualized Education Plan) support
• Homework and academic support in special education
• School readiness programs for younger children

🎒 School Integration: Our goal is mainstream school success. We work closely with schools in Visakhapatnam to ensure your child gets appropriate support in the classroom.

What should I expect in the first session?

+

The first session is an assessment and consultation where we understand your child's needs and create a personalized therapy plan.

First visit includes:
1. Parent Interview: Discuss concerns, medical history, developmental milestones
2. Child Assessment: Standardized tests and observations (play-based, non-stressful)
3. Goal Setting: Identify priorities and create therapy objectives
4. Treatment Plan: Recommend therapy type, frequency, and duration
5. Questions: Time for all your concerns and doubts

⏱️ Duration: First assessment takes 60-90 minutes. Bring any previous reports, school records, or medical documents. Children can bring a favorite toy for comfort!

Do you provide parent training and home programs?

+

Yes! Parent involvement is crucial for success. We provide comprehensive parent training and home activity programs.

Parent support includes:
• Weekly home activity sheets with specific exercises
• Monthly parent training sessions
• Video demonstrations of techniques
• WhatsApp support for daily questions
• Parent counseling for emotional support
• Sibling involvement strategies

👨‍👩‍👧 Family-Centered Approach: Research shows children progress 3x faster when families practice at home. We empower parents to become co-therapists in their child's development journey.

What age groups do you work with?

+

We serve children from 18 months to 16 years, with specialized programs for different age groups.

Age-specific programs:
• Toddlers (18 months - 3 years): Early intervention, speech stimulation, sensory play
• Preschool (3-6 years): School readiness, social skills, communication development
• School Age (6-12 years): Academic support, social skills, independence training
• Teens (12-16 years): Life skills, vocational training, social communication

🌱 Early Intervention: The earlier we start, the better! Brain plasticity is highest in early years, making therapy most effective for younger children.
