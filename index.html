Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

What is Speech Therapy and when should I start? +

Speech therapy helps children develop communication skills including speaking, understanding language, and social communication. Early intervention is key!

Start speech therapy if your child:

  • Isn't babbling by 12 months
  • Has limited words by 18 months
  • Isn't combining words by 2 years
  • Has unclear speech by 3 years
  • Struggles to follow simple instructions

The earlier you start, the better the outcomes. Our RCI-registered therapists in Visakhapatnam provide comprehensive assessments and personalized treatment plans.

Is online therapy as effective as in-person sessions? +

Yes! Online therapy is highly effective and research-backed.

✅ 85% Success Rate: Studies show online therapy achieves similar outcomes to in-person sessions for most conditions.

Benefits of Online Therapy:

  • Convenience: No travel time, flexible scheduling
  • Consistency: Fewer missed sessions due to weather/traffic
  • Natural Environment: Child learns in their comfortable home setting
  • Parent Involvement: Easier for parents to observe and participate
  • Technology Engagement: Kids often respond well to screen-based activities

Best For: Speech therapy, parent training, behavioral consultations, follow-up sessions

When In-Person is Better: Initial assessments, hands-on sensory work, complex motor activities

We offer hybrid models combining both online and in-person sessions for optimal results!

How does Occupational Therapy help my child? +

Occupational Therapy (OT) helps children develop skills needed for daily activities and school success.

OT addresses:

  • Fine Motor Skills: Writing, buttoning, using utensils
  • Gross Motor Skills: Balance, coordination, strength
  • Sensory Processing: Managing sensory input (sounds, textures, movement)
  • Self-Care: Dressing, eating, hygiene
  • Visual-Motor Skills: Hand-eye coordination, copying shapes
  • Attention & Focus: Sitting still, completing tasks

Our OT sessions in Visakhapatnam use play-based activities to make therapy fun and engaging while building essential life skills.

What is ABA Therapy and who needs it? +

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) is an evidence-based therapy that teaches skills and reduces challenging behaviors through positive reinforcement.

ABA helps with:

  • Communication and language skills
  • Social interaction and play skills
  • Daily living skills (eating, dressing, toileting)
  • Reducing problem behaviors (tantrums, aggression, self-injury)
  • Academic and pre-academic skills
  • Attention and focus

Who benefits from ABA?

While commonly used for autism spectrum disorder, ABA therapy also helps children with ADHD, developmental delays, behavioral challenges, and learning disabilities.

Our ABA programs in Vizag are individualized and family-centered, with regular progress monitoring.

How long does therapy take to show results? +

Every child is unique, but here's what to typically expect:

Initial Changes: 4-8 weeks - Small improvements in targeted skills
Noticeable Progress: 3-6 months - Clear skill development
Significant Gains: 6-12 months - Major milestones achieved

Factors affecting progress:

  • Severity of the condition
  • Frequency of therapy sessions (2-3x/week recommended)
  • Home practice and parent involvement
  • Child's age (earlier intervention = faster progress)
  • Consistency of attendance

We provide progress reports every 3 months to track your child's development and adjust therapy goals accordingly.

What are sensory integration issues? +

Sensory integration is how the brain processes information from our senses (touch, sound, sight, taste, smell, movement, body position).

Signs of sensory processing difficulties:

  • Over-responsive: Covers ears for normal sounds, dislikes certain textures, avoids messy play
  • Under-responsive: Doesn't notice pain, seeks intense sensory input, constantly moving
  • Sensory seeking: Crashes into things, chews on objects, loves spinning
  • Poor coordination: Clumsy, difficulty with balance, avoids playground equipment

How we help:

Our occupational therapists use sensory integration therapy with specialized equipment (swings, weighted items, textured materials) to help children process sensory information better and respond appropriately.

What happens in the first session? +

Your first visit is all about understanding your child's needs:

Step 1: Parent Interview (15-20 minutes)

  • Discuss your concerns and goals
  • Review developmental history
  • Understand family dynamics and daily routines

Step 2: Child Assessment (30-45 minutes)

  • Observe your child in play and structured activities
  • Conduct standardized assessments appropriate for age
  • Evaluate specific areas of concern

Step 3: Feedback & Planning (15-20 minutes)

  • Share initial findings and recommendations
  • Discuss therapy frequency and duration
  • Create a customized treatment plan
  • Answer all your questions
Total Duration: 60-90 minutes
What to Bring: Any previous reports, medical records, school reports

After the first session: You'll receive a detailed assessment report within 3-5 days with specific therapy recommendations.
