Our Therapy Services

🎯 Occupational Therapy (OT)

Helping children develop essential life skills through play-based activities. We focus on fine motor skills, sensory processing, and daily living activities to build independence and confidence.

💬 Speech Therapy

Supporting communication development through engaging, child-centered approaches. We work on articulation, language comprehension, social communication, and alternative communication methods.

🧩 Behavioral Therapy

Evidence-based strategies to support positive behaviors and emotional regulation. We create individualized plans that help children thrive in home, school, and community settings.

🎨 Special Education

Customized learning programs that meet each child where they are. We adapt teaching methods to match individual learning styles and developmental needs.

🏃 Physiotherapy

Building strength, coordination, and motor skills through movement-based therapy. We help children improve physical abilities and participate more fully in daily activities.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Training & Counseling

Empowering families with practical strategies and emotional support. We believe parents are the most important part of a child's therapy journey.

About Butterfly Bloom CDC

At Butterfly Bloom Child Development Center, we believe every child has the potential to flourish—they just need the right environment and support to bloom at their own unique pace.

Founded with a deep commitment to neurodivergent children and their families, we provide compassionate, evidence-based therapy services in Visakhapatnam. Our team of experienced therapists specializes in working with children with autism spectrum disorder, ADHD, sensory processing challenges, and developmental delays.

We take a holistic, family-centered approach. We don't just work with children—we partner with parents, caregivers, and educators to create consistent support systems that extend beyond our therapy rooms.

Our Philosophy: Every child is unique. Every family's journey is different. We celebrate neurodiversity and focus on building on each child's strengths while gently supporting areas of growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you work with?

We work with children from 18 months to 14 years old. Early intervention makes a significant difference, but we're here to support children at any stage of their development journey.

How do I know if my child needs therapy?

If you've noticed delays in speech, difficulty with social interactions, challenges with daily activities, sensory sensitivities, or behavioral concerns, a consultation can help. Trust your instincts—you know your child best.

Are your therapists qualified and certified?

Our team brings together professionals registered with the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI), with diverse experience from Vizag, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kerala, and even international exposure from the USA and UK. We've worked with over 500 families in and around Visakhapatnam. What sets us apart isn't just our qualifications—it's our genuine passion for helping children thrive and our commitment to staying current with the latest therapeutic approaches.

Do you offer online therapy sessions?

Yes! We provide both in-person and online therapy sessions. Online sessions work particularly well for parent training, speech therapy, and behavioral consultations, making quality care accessible no matter where you are.

How long does therapy take?

Every child's journey is unique. Some children make rapid progress in a few months, while others benefit from longer-term support. We regularly assess progress and adjust our approach to ensure we're meeting your child's evolving needs.

How much does therapy cost and do you accept insurance?

We create personalized therapy plans based on your child's specific needs and your family's goals. Since our online sessions often involve both parent training and direct work with your child, the time commitment can vary. We offer flexible monthly packages to make therapy more accessible. Many families also find that their insurance providers offer reimbursement for therapy services. We're happy to discuss what might work best for your situation—just reach out and let's have a conversation.

Can parents observe therapy sessions?

Absolutely! We encourage parent involvement. You're welcome to observe sessions, and we'll regularly share strategies you can use at home to reinforce your child's progress.

What should I bring to the first appointment?

Bring any previous assessment reports, medical records, school reports, and a list of your concerns and goals. Most importantly, bring your questions—we're here to listen and support you.
