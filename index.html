About Butterfly Bloom CDC

At Butterfly Bloom Child Development Center in Visakhapatnam, we believe every child deserves the opportunity to reach their full potential. Our RCI-registered center provides comprehensive developmental support through evidence-based therapies and personalized care.

With over 8 years of experience serving families in Vizag, our team of certified therapists specializes in Speech Therapy, Occupational Therapy, ABA Therapy, and Special Education. We create individualized treatment plans that celebrate each child's unique strengths and address their specific needs.

Services We Offer

Speech Therapy

Expert speech and language intervention for articulation, fluency, voice disorders, and communication delays. Our certified speech therapists use play-based and evidence-based techniques.

Occupational Therapy

Comprehensive OT services focusing on fine motor skills, sensory integration, self-care activities, and daily living skills to enhance independence and confidence.

ABA Therapy

Applied Behavior Analysis therapy for children with autism and developmental delays. Structured programs to improve social skills, communication, and adaptive behaviors.

Special Education

Sensory Integration
